Patient Info

Physiotherapy in Nepal: Common Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from patients in Kathmandu and Lalitpur.

Do I need a doctor's referral to see a physiotherapist?

No. You can book a physiotherapy assessment at Walk Nepal directly without a referral. If your condition needs medical input, we will guide you on the right next step.

What conditions do you treat at your Lalitpur clinic?

We treat back and neck pain, sports injuries, post-surgery recovery, stroke and neurological conditions, joint and posture problems, and pediatric movement delays — with a personalized plan for each patient.

How long is a physiotherapy session?

Your first visit includes a full assessment and usually takes 45–60 minutes. Follow-up treatment sessions are typically 30–45 minutes depending on your recovery plan.

Where is Walk Nepal located and what are your hours?

We are in Lalitpur, opposite Santa Nirankari Bhawan, Kathmandu. We are open Sunday to Friday, 7 AM–6 PM, and Saturday 9 AM–5 PM. You can call or message us on WhatsApp to book.

How soon can I get an appointment?

Same-week evaluations are usually available. Request an appointment through the form on this page or message us on WhatsApp for the fastest response during clinic hours.
