Early intervention

The earlier a child starts, the further they get

Early identification and treatment of developmental delay is critical. The sooner a child receives support, the greater the chance of improvement in language, motor coordination and cognitive function.

  • Not sitting, standing or walking on time Motor delays respond well to early, consistent physiotherapy.
  • Few or no words by two years Speech therapy can start long before a child is verbal.
  • Little eye contact or response to name Worth an assessment, whatever the eventual diagnosis.
  • Distress with touch, sound or textures Sensory integration therapy is designed for this.

Waiting to "see if it passes" costs time that a child can use. If something feels off, book an assessment.

Questions parents ask

Before your first visit

What conditions does Walk Nepal treat?

In children: cerebral palsy, global developmental delay, autism spectrum disorder, ADHD, intellectual disability, Down syndrome and muscular dystrophy. In adults: stroke, brain and spine injury, movement disorders, neuropathy, myopathy, motor neurone disease and orthopedic rehabilitation.

At what age should a child start therapy for a developmental delay?

As soon as you notice a delay. Early identification and treatment matter — the earlier a child receives support, the greater the chance of improvement in language, motor coordination and cognitive skills. We assess infants, toddlers and school-age children.

Do I need a doctor's referral to book an appointment?

No. You can book an assessment at Walk Nepal directly, without a referral. If your child or family member needs medical input alongside therapy, we will tell you what to do next.

How long is a therapy session?

The first visit is a full assessment and usually takes 45 to 60 minutes. Follow-up sessions are typically 30 to 45 minutes, one-to-one with your therapist. How often you come depends on the treatment plan.

Can parents stay in the room during therapy?

Yes. Parent education and support is part of how we work. You are welcome to stay, and your therapist will show you the exercises and routines to continue at home between sessions.

Where is Walk Nepal, and when are you open?

We are in Lalitpur, opposite Santa Nirankari Bhawan, in the Kathmandu Valley. Open Sunday to Friday, 7 AM–6 PM, and Saturday 9 AM–5 PM. Call 9840705523 or message us on WhatsApp to book.
