What neurological rehabilitation is

Neurological physiotherapy supports people whose movement is affected by a condition of the nervous system. It works on balance, coordination, strength, and the practical tasks of daily life.

Who it may help

This service may be appropriate for people experiencing changes in balance, walking, coordination, or day-to-day movement following a neurological condition. A physiotherapist will confirm whether it suits your situation during your assessment.

Conditions commonly assessed

  • Balance and mobility difficulties
  • Weakness affecting movement
  • Coordination problems
  • Difficulty walking safely

When to seek urgent medical care

Physiotherapy is appropriate for many musculoskeletal and movement problems, but some symptoms need urgent medical assessment first.

[CLINICIAN REVIEW REQUIRED: confirm and localise the red-flag list below for Nepal, including the correct emergency contact and nearest hospital guidance]

  • Sudden loss of bladder or bowel control, or numbness around the groin or inner thighs.
  • Progressive weakness, numbness, or loss of coordination in the arms or legs.
  • Severe pain after a significant fall, accident, or trauma.
  • Fever, unexplained weight loss, or night pain alongside your symptoms.
  • Chest pain, severe headache, difficulty speaking, or facial drooping — call local emergency services immediately.
